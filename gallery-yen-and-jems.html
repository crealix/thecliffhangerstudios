RAW. CANDID. REAL.
That’s exactly how Yen and Jems wanted their wedding video to be. No staged glam shots, no scripted poses—just pure, unfiltered moments. They wanted to relive their day as it truly happened, to feel every heartbeat, every tear, and every burst of laughter when they hit play years from now.
Last Thursday, the rain poured relentlessly. We all hoped for sunshine. But love, much like their story, was unstoppable. Yen and Jems embraced the day as it came, trusting in God’s perfect timing. And just before the ceremony began, the skies cleared—only to welcome the rain again near the end, as if sealing their vows with a heavenly blessing.
The rain, the delays, the detours—every twist and turn was a part of their journey, a testament to their faith and unwavering love. Through three years of challenges, they stood strong. And on this day, they won.
Jems and Yen, we are beyond honored to have captured your love story. May your marriage be filled with endless blessings, unwavering faith, and a love that shines through every storm.
