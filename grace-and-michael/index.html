FAQs

What’s the venue address? +
The ceremony and reception will be held at:

What is the Dress code? +
The dress code is *formal / semi-formal attire*.

Ladies: Long or cocktail dress
Gentlemen: Long or short sleeves polo and slacks or chinos
Is there a theme for the wedding? +
We encourage guests to incorporate earth tone warm greens into their outfits.
Will the ceremony and reception be indoors or outdoors? +
The ceremony will be taking place in an outdoor garden; we recommend flat-soled shoes or wedges to avoid sinking into the grass. The reception will be held indoors.
What happens after the ceremony? +
After the ceremony, the wedding party will be taking pictures nearby for around an hour.  Guests can head straight to the reception hall, where we will be serving finger foods and beverages during a cocktail hour.
What time should I arrive? +
Please arrive 15–30 minutes early. The ceremony will begin promptly at 4:00 PM.
You can also add: "Late arrivals may be asked to wait until after the ceremony."
Is there a parking space for my car? +
Yes, there is a parking area available in the venue.
Are kids invited? +
We love your little ones, but this will be an adults-only celebration. Thank you for understanding!
Can I bring a plus one? +
We have a strict guest list and we might not have any extra seats for your companion.

Please understand that this event is strictly by invitation only. Names that not found on the guest list we have provided to our Coordinator will not be allowed to enter.
Can I sit anywhere during the reception? +
We planned a seating arrangement for our guests’ convenience and group familiarity. Please follow accordingly. 
Our coordinators will assist you in finding your designated seats.
Feel free to ask them for assistance and they will gladly help you.
Will there be dinner served? +
Absolutely—we’ll be serving a delicious buffet meal.
Is RSVP required? +
YES, please!
Confirming your attendance will help us include you in our guest list.
Please let us know before November 25, 2025.
Can I take photos during the ceremony? +
We’re having an unplugged ceremony — please turn off phones and cameras until after we say “I do.” 📵
